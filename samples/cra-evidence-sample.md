Adapted from the existing CRA kit's Evidence and Decision Log. All product, people, event and evidence references below are synthetic. This is not customer evidence, a completed assessment or a legal determination.

A scanner result starts an investigation; it does not establish what happened in your product. This excerpt shows how to keep observations separate from decisions while that investigation is still open.

The real record should identify retained source evidence, the actual people making decisions and the basis for those decisions. This example does not set an awareness timestamp or calculate a filing deadline.

The [European Commission's current reporting guidance](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-reporting) distinguishes the early-warning and notification deadlines from the final-report deadlines. For actively exploited vulnerabilities, the final-report deadline depends on corrective-measure availability. For severe incidents, it is linked to the 72-hour notification. Check the applicable obligation and current primary guidance before filing.
